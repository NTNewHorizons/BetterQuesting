[C]You've reached the end.
[C]Well done.

But I want to talk to you not about that.
Not about victory. Not about the end. Not about the game.
About why all of this exists at all.

You see, I'm not a god.
Not a genius. Not a great creator.
I'm just a person. Just like you.
Maybe even worse.

I don't know how to live correctly.
I don't know what the meaning is.
I just do what I can.
And that's enough.

Have you ever wondered...
who guided your steps?
Who whispered to you: "§f§k§a§bone more step§r"?
You could have stopped. At any moment.
But you didn't stop.
And maybe that's exactly why you're here now.

I didn't stop once either.
I could have given up. I could have grown bitter.
But I made something of my own.
And here's why.

The world isn't always kind.
You know that. I know you know.
Sometimes it becomes so harsh that it feels like there's simply no other world.
Too much noise. Too much anger.
Too much pain that one person cannot fix.

I've seen it. Not in movies. Not in the news.
I know what it's like to lose a home.
I know what it's like to fear for the ones you love.
I know what it's like to feel like a stranger in your own life.

I know what it's like when your only friend is a game.
Because once, a game was my only friend.
It understood me. And I didn't understand it. Not yet.

And when the world becomes too heavy,
a person doesn't have many ways out left.
You can give up.
You can grow bitter.
Or you can make something of your own.
I chose the third.

Not because I was sure it would change the world.
Not because I dreamed of greatness.
I just wanted to create a place where you can forget about everything else for a while.
A place you can come to after a hard day.
Sit down. Exhale. And just... be.

Because home isn't always walls.
Sometimes home is those who understand you without words.
Who don't shout. Those next to whom you don't have to pretend.
If you have people like that - take care of them.
And become that for someone yourself.

I had cats. Two. Timofey and Margot.
They understood me better than some people.
But they're far away. Like everything I love.

I don't have many people who understand me without words.
I've lost many.
Some moved away. Some disappeared. Some simply stopped answering.
But those who stayed - they're worth everything.

Because sometimes a person doesn't need to save the whole world.
Sometimes it's enough to give them a place where they feel at home.

There's always enough bad in the world as it is.
You don't need to create more of it.
But happiness - there's a shortage.
Everything else can be found. Produced. Bought.
But happiness can't be produced.
It can only be given.

And here's the most important thing.
Listen.
§dLove.§r

Not necessarily someone in particular.
Love people. Love moments. Love what you do.
Because everything in this world that's truly worth something -
is made out of love.

Sometimes the ones we love are far away. Sometimes - very far.
Sometimes they leave, and you can't call them back anymore.
But love doesn't know how to measure distance.
It remains.
In people. In the things you left them. In places where someone was happy.

I know what it's like when a person simply disappears from your life.
And you can't do anything about it.
You just... wait.
And hope that one day they'll return.

Yes, it hurts. I know.
Sometimes it seems safer not to love anyone at all.
But think: pain is the price for being able to feel at all.
And it's the best deal in the world.

I'm telling you all this not for no reason.
I'm telling you because you're here.
Not as a player. Not as a name on a list.
Not as just another person on the other side of the screen.
You are the reason all of this makes sense.
Without you, it would just be lines, files, and emptiness.

Now you're here, PLAYERNAME.
And this place is no longer mine.
It's ours.

But here's the thing.
I don't know you.
I don't know your time of day. I don't know your story.
It might be morning for you. It's evening for me.
And we're both right.

We tamed time. Put it on a chain of clocks.
But it's like mercury.
Break it - and it gathers back together. Whole. Unbroken.

Don't wear time on your wrist like a chain.
Don't count the days.
§eLive.§r

And I also know what everyone who lives long enough knows.
One day you'll leave.
I'll leave too. Everyone will leave.
Worlds will vanish. The light will go out. Names will be forgotten.

When a person dies, their world dies.
All of it. Entirely.
So while you're here - your world is alive.

Scary? Me too.
But that's exactly why it's so important right now.

While you're here - you can leave something behind.
Not necessarily great. Not necessarily famous.
Sometimes one thing that someone will remember someday is enough.
One smile. One kind deed.
One place where someone was happy.

You can.
And no one can do it instead of you.

People tell me: soon machines will replace humans.
I don't believe it.
A machine can remember everything.
But it can't want.
It guesses the next word.
But a real thing is born when a person cannot help but make it.

So create.
§6You are irreplaceable.§r

So.
If you ever get the chance to make this world even a little better -
do it.
Not because you have to.
But because you can.

If something remains after you -
let it not be fear. Not anger. Not destruction.
Let something good remain.

And when someone asks why you did all this -
let the answer be simple:
"Because I wanted someone to feel a little better."

You walked this path to the end.
But remember one thing.
This project is not the meaning.
It is a consequence of meaning.
